# Calculus

## Overview

This section explores the mathematical study of limits, continuity, differentiation and integration, with emphasis on their theoretical foundations and practical applications.

## Topics

- Limits and continuity
- Differentiation techniques
- Applications of derivatives
- Partial derivatives
- Indefinite and definite integration
- Integration techniques
- Multiple integrals
- Improper integrals

## Applications

Calculus will be applied to rates of change, optimization, marginal cost and revenue, economic analysis and continuous mathematical models.
