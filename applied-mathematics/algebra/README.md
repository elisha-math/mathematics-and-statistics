# Algebra

## Overview

This section explores advanced algebraic concepts, methods of solving equations and inequalities, and the mathematical foundations of sequences and series.

## Topics

- Polynomial and rational equations
- Simultaneous equations
- Linear, quadratic and higher-order inequalities
- Arithmetic and geometric sequences
- Finite and infinite series
- Mathematical induction
- Exponential and logarithmic equations

## Applications

Algebraic methods will be applied to mathematical problem-solving, economic relationships, financial calculations and other real-world problems.
