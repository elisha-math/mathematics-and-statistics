# Descriptive Statistics

## Introduction

Descriptive statistics help us organise, summarise, and understand data. Instead of looking at a long list of numbers, we can use a few statistical measures to describe the main features of a dataset.

In this project, I explore some basic measures of descriptive statistics using a small dataset.

## Dataset

The following values represent the marks scored by 10 students in a mathematics test:

`45, 50, 55, 60, 60, 65, 70, 75, 80, 90`

## 1. Mean

The mean is the average of all the values in a dataset.

\[
\text{Mean}=\frac{\text{Sum of all values}}{\text{Number of values}}
\]

\[
=\frac{650}{10}=65
\]

**Mean = 65**

## 2. Median

The median is the middle value when the data is arranged in ascending or descending order.

Since there are 10 observations, the median is the average of the 5th and 6th values.

\[
\text{Median}=\frac{60+65}{2}=62.5
\]

**Median = 62.5**

## 3. Mode

The mode is the value that appears most frequently in a dataset.

In this dataset, 60 appears twice, while the other values appear once.

**Mode = 60**

## 4. Range

The range measures the difference between the highest and lowest values.

\[
\text{Range}=90-45=45
\]

**Range = 45**

## 5. Variance and Standard Deviation

Variance measures how far the observations are spread around the mean. Standard deviation is the square root of the variance.

For this example, treating the 10 students as the entire population:

\[
\sigma^2=\frac{\sum(x-\mu)^2}{N}
\]

The sum of squared deviations from the mean is 1,900.

\[
\sigma^2=\frac{1900}{10}=190
\]

\[
\sigma=\sqrt{190}\approx13.78
\]

**Population variance = 190**

**Population standard deviation ≈ 13.78**

## Conclusion

The average mark in this dataset is 65, while the median is 62.5 and the mode is 60. The range of 45 shows the difference between the highest and lowest marks.

The standard deviation of approximately 13.78 indicates how far the marks typically vary around the mean.

This example demonstrates how descriptive statistics can turn a collection of raw numbers into information that is easier to understand and interpret.
